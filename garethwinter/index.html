THE STORY

Cycling isn't my content niche. It's my family trade.

My grandfather raced time trials in post-war Britain, training by feel between RAF shifts and riding 88 miles each way to court my grandmother. My dad built wheels and led the Surrey League. I grew up on the back of his tandem, then spent ten years inside Team Sky and British Cycling, in the rooms where marginal gains was still a working method, before it became a slogan, from Bradley Wiggins's Hour Record trackside to the daily grind of the biggest team in the sport.

Since then I've been creative director at Veloforte, the UK's fastest-growing sports nutrition brand, and creative director then head of content at Mindvalley, the world's largest personal growth platform, making campaigns and films watched by millions. This year I founded Forma, an AI cycling coach, because the system that builds champions should fit in a jersey pocket.
